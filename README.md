Official developer repository and documentation for [Royaltify](https://royaltify.in) — India's premier ₹0 upfront music distribution and royalty management platform.

### Core Mission
Dismantling gatekeeping in the South Asian music industry by providing independent musicians, beatmakers, and record labels with 100% master rights retention, free ISRC/UPC generation, caller tune delivery (JioTune & Airtel HelloTune), and direct INR payouts via UPI/NEFT.
